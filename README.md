### 🔬 What I do

- **Analytics & BI** — I build dashboards for programme and operational KPIs, automated reporting in SQL and Excel, and survey analysis that ends in a clear recommendation.
- **Machine learning & computer vision** — detection, classification and segmentation models for medical images (MRI, blood-film microscopy, retinal scans), from data validation and labelling to training and evaluation.
- **Data governance & quality** — cataloguing, quality checks, interoperability and responsible data sharing for government data systems.

Most of my work starts with a question from someone who isn't a data person — a ministry planner, a programme manager, a clinician. I scope it, check the data is fit for it, do the analysis, and hand back something they can use.
